THEINE
======

INSTALLING
Open Theine right from this window. It moves itself to the Applications
folder and restarts from there. Without administrator rights, Theine goes
to the Applications folder inside your home folder. Once it runs, its icon
appears in the menu bar at the top right of the screen.

You can also install Theine by hand. Drag its icon onto the Applications
folder next to it and launch Theine from the Applications folder.

IF macOS WON'T OPEN THE APP
The app isn't notarized by Apple, so on the first launch macOS may show
a warning ("cannot verify the developer" or "Apple could not verify it is free
of malware"). You only need to allow it once.

  • On macOS 15 and later, close the warning with "Done", open System
    Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway"
    next to Theine and confirm.

  • On macOS 14, right-click Theine in Finder, choose "Open", then click
    "Open" again in the warning.

  • If that didn't help, drag Theine onto the Applications folder, open
    Terminal and run:
        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Theine.app

WHAT THEINE DOES
Theine keeps your Mac from falling asleep while you are busy: a download, a
build, a presentation or a long calculation. The cup in the menu bar is empty
while the Mac may sleep and full while Theine keeps it awake.

THE MENU BAR ICON
Any click on the icon opens the menu. It has "Turn On" or "Turn Off" (the
Shift-Command-T label on that item works while the menu is open), the
"Keep the display on" and "Stay awake with the lid closed" checkmarks and
"Turn On For" with "Indefinitely", 5, 15, 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4 and 8 hours.
While the mode is on and "Keep the display on" is checked, the display
doesn't dim or go dark and the screen saver doesn't start. Without it the
display goes dark as usual and the Mac keeps running. The checkmarks take
effect at once, also while the mode is on. A line under them shows the
state: "On for 12 min" or "40 min left". Then come Settings, Check for
Updates, About and Quit.

When the time is up, Theine turns itself off and plays a short sound if sound
effects are on in Settings.

THE CLOSED LID
With "Stay awake with the lid closed" (checked from the start) the Mac
doesn't sleep even with its lid closed, on battery and on power. For this
Theine installs a small helper, and macOS asks for an administrator
password once, the first time the mode is turned on. If you say no,
everything else works, and the menu gets an "Allow…" item to install the
helper later. With the lid closed the Mac runs warmer, so don't put it in
a bag while Theine is on. When Theine is turned off or quits, the helper
puts the sleep setting back within seconds.

SETTINGS
Settings (Command-,) has three switches for Theine and a row for a shortcut.
"Stay awake with the lid closed" is the same as the checkmark in the menu.
"Turn off below 20% battery" turns Theine off when the Mac runs
on battery and the charge falls below 20%, and stops it from turning on at that
charge until the Mac is plugged in. "Turn on at launch" turns Theine on with no
time limit at every launch. The "Shortcut for turning on" row records your own
keys, which must include Command, Control or Option. They turn Theine on and
off in any app. There is no shortcut by default, so Theine takes no keys from
other apps.

If Theine restarts by itself, for example to update, the mode carries on with
the same time. "Quit Theine" in the menu and restarting the Mac turn it off.

LANGUAGE
Theine works in Russian and English. In Settings (⌘,) choose "Same as
System", "Русский" or "English". With "Same as System" Theine takes the
first suitable language from the list in System Settings > General >
Language & Region. A new language takes effect after a restart, and the
"Restart" button appears right under the choice.

UPDATES
Theine looks for a new version on GitHub at launch, every three hours and
after the Mac wakes up. It downloads a new version by itself, checks its
signature and installs it when Theine is idle. While Theine keeps the Mac
awake with the lid closed, the new version waits until the mode is turned
off. With "Install updates
automatically" turned off in Settings, the icon gets a dot instead, and the
menu gets a "Version ... is available" item that opens a card with "Later" and
"Update".

OPEN AT LOGIN
Theine opens at login to install updates on time and, with "Turn on at
launch", to keep the Mac awake right away. To stop it, turn off "Open at
login" in Settings.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 14 or later, a Mac with Apple Silicon or Intel.
